## Why I built it

I wanted to learn real time 3D graphics by building something that moves. A
solar system covers a lot of ground: textured spheres, a scene graph, nested
rotations, and an animation loop that has to stay smooth.

## How it works

- **A Three.js scene graph.** The sun, the planets, and their orbits are nested
  `THREE.Group`s, so rotating a parent group carries its planet around the sun.
- **Different orbit speeds.** Each planet's speed comes from an `EARTH_YEAR`
  constant (Mercury fastest, Mars slowest), driven by a `requestAnimationFrame`
  loop.
- **Small reusable classes.** `Planet` and `Rotation` handle geometry, texture
  loading, and spin, so adding a new body takes a couple of lines.
- **Live controls.** A `dat.gui` panel turns each planet's orbit on and off while
  the scene runs.

## Stack

Three.js / WebGL · Next.js + React · texture mapped `SphereGeometry` ·
`dat.gui`.

## What I took from it

My first real project on the 3D side of the web: scene graphs, texture loading,
and animation loops.
