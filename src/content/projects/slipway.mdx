## The idea

Slipway runs on a schedule. It looks at what's new in tech, combines that with
my own context, and suggests software that might be worth building. It rates
each idea, and for the ones I approve it hands the work to a team of AI agents.

## The part I care most about: verification

This comes straight out of my day job in QA. AI coding agents do real work, but
they're overconfident and can't be trusted to say when they're done. So Slipway
has one hard rule: it never ships on its own. Every task has to pass a gate
before a person looks at it.

The gate produces a Proof of Work bundle that answers one question: did the
agent do what it said, and can I check that without redoing it? The bundle
holds the test output, the exact diff, before and after screenshots, the
commands the agent ran with their exit codes, independent check results, and
where it all came from (model, prompt hash, git SHA). A reviewer can approve
quickly, and the bundle stays around as an audit trail.

## Seeing the whole team

You can follow the work at every level, from a board and backlog across several
projects down to the proof for a single task and whether it passed its gate.

## Where it is now

In progress. The verification gate and the Proof of Work format are the core of
it, and I keep building on them.
