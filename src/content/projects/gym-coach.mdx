## The problem

Most workout trackers log what you did and stop there. They never tell you what
to do next, and they're easy to abandon after a week. I wanted a coach that plans
the session, makes logging quick, and adjusts the program based on how training
actually went.

## What I'm building

- **A program that adapts.** Progressive overload, deloads, and missed sessions
  are handled in code, so the next session's targets come from recent history.
- **Quick logging.** Recording sets, reps, and weight should take as few taps as
  possible, since friction is what kills tracking apps.

## Status

In progress. It's one of the personal tools I build to make my own routines run
smoother.
