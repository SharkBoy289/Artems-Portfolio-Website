## The idea

Grow a squishy blob from bite size to building size. Food spawns around a park,
players race to grab it, and anyone can slap a rival to steal what they're
carrying. Feeding unlocks mutations, and fusing two blobs makes a new creature
that goes into your personal Blobdex.

## How it's built

I'm building it with a collaborator, Ed, in Roblox Studio with Luau.

- **Server-authoritative game loop.** Currency, feeding, pen ownership and
  purchases are decided on the server, and every client request is validated,
  so a modified client can't hand itself food or coins.
- **Growth capped by pen size.** Your blob physically grows on the map until it
  fills its pen, which makes pen upgrades the main progression path.
- **Mobile-first controls** for short sessions, since most of the audience
  plays on phones.
